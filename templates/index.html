How KisanDwar Works

Seamless transparent procurement from farmer field to direct bank account

1

📅 Slot & Bhoomi Quota

Farmer selects commodity and time slot. Bhoomi Land Records verify genuine cultivation quota (25 Qtl/acre) and capture live grain photos to block middlemen.

2

🎟️ Digital QR & SMS Pass

Farmer receives instant encrypted QR Entry Pass and bilingual SMS. On arrival, gate camera scans QR and triggers token sequence without physical queueing.

3

⚖️ Weighment & Weather Shield

Trolley is weighed at digital scale with live moisture sensor. Weather Sentinel checks rain radar and triggers automated tarpaulin covering alerts.

4

💳 Direct DBT & e-J-Form

Digitally signed e-J-Form is issued instantly. Payment is automatically pushed to the farmer's Aadhaar-seeded bank account via PFMS DBT with zero delay.

🧮 Bhoomi Anti-Hoarding Quota

Cross-validates acreage and prevents hoarders or traders from dumping black-market grains under farmer identities.

☔ Unseasonal Rain Sentinel

Monitors IMD rainfall forecasts and manages tarpaulin covers at procurement yards to guarantee zero grain rotting.

🗣️ 5-Language Audio & PA

Voice Assistant and Yard Loudspeaker announcements in Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and English for total rural literacy.

🌐 Offline PWA Village Ready

Local cache and offline-first queue tokens enable full functionality in remote villages even with zero cellular signal.
